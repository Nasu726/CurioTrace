Why CurioTrace asks for website access

This PoC needs permission to read ordinary HTTP/HTTPS pages while a recording session is active so it can follow browsing across sites and reconstruct what was actually visible.

  • Installing the extension alone does not start capture.
  • Private/incognito and excluded pages remain outside the intended capture scope.
  • The permission is not consent to send page contents to an external model.
  • If you decline the website-access permission, the recording test will not start.
